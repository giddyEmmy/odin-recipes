TikTok Pink Sauce

What is it?

Do you remember the infamous pink sauce from TikTok? If you don't know what i'm talking about, well... it's a sauce, and it went viral for... being pink! Recipe is from Yummy Tummy Aarthi this time!

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Pink Dragon Fruit Powder
  • 2 teaspoon Garlic Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Onion Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Dried Parsley
  • ½ teaspoon Red Chilli Flakes
  • 1 tablespoon Vinegar
  • 2 teaspoon Lemon Juice
  • Pink Salt to taste
  • 4 tablespoon Milk
  • 3 tablespoon Refined Oil
  • 2 tablespoon Honey
  • 4 tablespoon Water

How?

  1. Take all the ingredients in a blender jar or a bowl and blend till combined.

Awesum sauce! Enjoy!
